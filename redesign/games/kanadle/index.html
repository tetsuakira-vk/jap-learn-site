How to play

Guess the 5-kana Japanese word in 6 tries. After each guess the tiles change colour.

On-screen keyboard

Tap any kana to add it to your guess.

Press ゛ then a key for voiced sounds — か→が, さ→ざ, た→だ, は→ば.

Press ° then a key for the ぱ row — は→ぱ, ひ→ぴ etc.

Type with your keyboard

Type romaji and it converts to hiragana automatically:

ka→か   shi→し   tsu→つ

chi→ち   fu→ふ   ji→じ

Double a consonant for っ — kka→っか, tta→った.

Enter to submit   ⌫ to delete
