Mission

使命

Japanese Unlocked exists for one reason: to make Japanese genuinely learnable for anyone, at no cost. No subscriptions, no ad walls between you and content, no gamified dark patterns — just the vocabulary, grammar, kanji, and practice tools that actually work.

Japanese is one of the most rewarding languages an English speaker can learn. It's also one of the most misrepresented — surrounded by apps that turn it into a casual phone game and courses that cost hundreds of pounds to deliver what a well-organised free site can do better. This is that site.

What's on the site

サイトの内容

Japanese Unlocked covers the full path from zero to JLPT N1:

Scripts

Complete guides to Hiragana, Katakana, and Kanji — all five JLPT levels with readings, stroke notes, and audio. Every character playable at the click of a card.

Vocabulary

Searchable reference tools for numbers, counters, and everyday phrases. N5-specific vocabulary with audio, organised by semantic category. The dictionary covers 500+ words searchable by Japanese, romaji, or English.

Grammar

Particle guides, verb conjugation tables, and pattern references for N5–N4 grammar. A sentence quiz to test your knowledge under pressure.

JLPT

Dedicated study guides for every level (N5–N1) with stats, test format breakdowns, key grammar areas, and study tips. Full mock tests in the format of the real exam.

Echo 影

Immersive audio conversation practice. Step into a scene — a ramen shop, a shrine, a bar — and work through a real exchange with a native-voiced character. 37 situations across N5 and N4.

Games & Practice

Kanji Quest, Memory Match, RAPID — actual games, not padded learning exercises. Plus Sentence Forge for grammar pattern writing and the Immersive Reader for reading comprehension.

Philosophy

考え方

Free means free

Everything on this site is free. Not "free tier with premium features" — fully free. There is no premium version. If we build it, it's on the site for everyone.

Audio matters

Every word, every grammar example, every kanji is playable. Japanese is a tonal, pitch-accented language — reading it without hearing it builds bad habits from day one. We make audio the default, not an add-on.

Honest about difficulty

Japanese takes a long time to learn. N1 takes most people four or more years of serious study. We don't promise fluency in thirty days or ten minutes a day. What we do promise is that everything on this site is the real thing — not simplified, not sanitised, not watered down to protect engagement metrics.

Reference over course

This isn't a course with a locked linear path. It's a reference — open, browsable, searchable. Come for a grammar point, stay for the kanji, leave when you've got what you needed. Japanese learning should fit around your life, not demand it on a schedule.

YouTube Channel

チャンネル
