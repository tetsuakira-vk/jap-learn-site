## What the videos cover

Most videos follow a simple format: pick a Japanese word or sentence, break down exactly what it means, and show how it's actually used in context.

For example — a video on **食べてみる** (*tabete miru*) wouldn't just give you "try eating." It would show you:

- What **食べて** is (the て-form of 食べる, *taberu* — to eat)
- What **みる** adds (literally "to try and see" — expressing having a go at something)
- Real example sentences: *寿司を食べてみました。* — "I tried eating sushi."
- When a Japanese speaker would actually use it vs. a simpler alternative

That kind of layered breakdown — meaning, structure, real usage — is what every video aims for.

**Topics covered so far:**
- Hiragana and katakana reading practice
- Core N5 and N4 vocabulary
- Everyday phrases for travel and conversation
- Grammar patterns with example sentences
- Kanji readings and how to remember them
